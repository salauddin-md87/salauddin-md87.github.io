ABOUT

Research at the intersection of materials, energy and intelligent systems

I am a Research Scientist in the Department of Nuclear, Plasma, and Radiological Engineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. My work connects functional materials and device engineering with practical sensing and monitoring technologies.

Over more than a decade of multidisciplinary research, I have developed and characterized triboelectric and hybrid electromagnetic energy harvesters, flexible and wearable electronics, MXene-based composites, smart textiles, and embedded sensing platforms. My approach spans materials design, device physics, experimental characterization, data acquisition, and systems integration.

At Illinois, I am advancing self-generating triboelectric sensors for thermal-hydraulic monitoring, with an emphasis on experimentally validated measurements, fault detection, and data-driven interpretation. Across application areas, I aim to translate promising device concepts into useful engineering instrumentation.

My research record includes 44 journal publications, 14 conference presentations, three patents, and six journal cover features, alongside collaborative research, student mentoring, and scholarly service.
